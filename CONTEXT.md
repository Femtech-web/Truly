# Truly Learning

Truly connects a learner's immediate goal with guided practice in the real application where the work happens.

## Language

**Learning goal**:
The outcome a learner wants, such as understanding Zama privacy or building a Nimiq Mini App.
_Avoid_: Prompt, topic

**Task**:
One learner-owned piece of active work toward a learning goal. A Task may be created directly by the learner or started from a Path.
_Avoid_: Skill, course

**Path**:
A creator-published, reusable learning product with ordered steps, resources, checks, a version and an optional price.
_Avoid_: Skill, tutorial, content pack

**Path step**:
One creator-authored stage within a Path, containing an outcome, guidance, optional workspace link and resources, a challenge and a completion rubric.
_Avoid_: Task, lesson

**Workspace link**:
The single reviewed destination that prepares the learner's current Task or Path step on their Mac. Opening it always remains an explicit learner action.
_Avoid_: Auto-open URL, launch command

**Resource**:
A reviewed supporting link attached to a Task or Path step. Resources are available from the Task panel but are never opened automatically.
_Avoid_: Attachment, content

**Learning session**:
One Task being practised on a chosen device, including its current step and progress.
_Avoid_: Skill runtime, desktop job

**Creator**:
A verified publisher who authors and maintains Paths and may receive payment for them.
_Avoid_: Tutor, seller

**Capability**:
The demonstrated ability a learner gains after completing a Task or Path.
_Avoid_: Skill record

**Skill**:
An ambiguous legacy term that can mean an ability, a Path or a Task. Do not use it in learner-facing language.
